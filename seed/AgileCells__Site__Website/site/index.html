Agile Cells

Many types of cells working together in an agile way.

An agile cell is a small team of people and agents, formed for a piece of work and ended after it, with its roles, its rules and its work written down as files anyone can read.

This site will explain the framework and publish the files that describe a cell. It is being written now, in the open.
